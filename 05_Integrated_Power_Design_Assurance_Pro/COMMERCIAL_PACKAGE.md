## 2. Why This is ProEngineering's Highest-Value Consulting Asset

Engineering consultancies and EPC firms routinely lose hundreds of thousands of dollars in liquidated damages, site rework, and lost client reputation due to simple coordination errors:
- A cable is ordered based on the original load schedule, but the process team upgraded the motor pump on the P&ID.
- An incoming breaker breaking capacity is calculated using estimated grid data, but the utility's actual short-circuit fault level is 20% higher.
- A bus coupler lacks interlocking logic, risking parallel transformer operation and switchgear arc-flash blowout.

`Integrated Power Design Assurance Pro` delivers **complete design risk mitigation** in hours rather than weeks of manual page-flipping.
