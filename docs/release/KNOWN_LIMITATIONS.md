# ProEngineering Electrical Intelligence Suite — Known Limitations & Scope Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0-rc1  
**Audience:** System Architects, Lead Electrical Engineers, Commercial Partners

---

## 1. Electrical & Mathematical Engine Limitations

1. **Topology Scope (Radial Only):**
   - The current calculation engine (`RADIAL_VD_IEC_V2`) supports strictly radial power distribution networks (source $\to$ bus $\to$ feeder $\to$ motor/static load).
   - Meshed, ring-main, or interconnected transmission/distribution networks requiring iterative Newton-Raphson or Fast Decoupled AC load flow solvers are not supported in v1.0 and are scheduled for Gate 4 / Gate 5 development.

2. **Phase Balance Assumption:**
   - Three-phase radial feeders assume symmetrical three-phase balanced loading ($I_a = I_b = I_c$). 
   - Phase imbalance calculations, zero-sequence neutral currents from unbalanced loads, and neutral conductor sizing under extreme triplen harmonic conditions ($>33\%$ THD) must be assessed as separate engineering exceptions.

3. **Adiabatic Short-Circuit Withstand ($t \le 5.0\text{ s}$):**
   - The adiabatic equation $S_{min} = (I_{sc} \cdot \sqrt{t}) / k$ per IEC 60364-5-54 assumes zero heat dissipation to the surroundings during the fault. This assumption is strictly valid for fault clearing times $t \le 5.0\text{ seconds}$.
   - For long clearing times ($t > 5.0\text{ s}$), non-adiabatic thermal dissipation models must be applied.

4. **Arc-Flash Incident Energy Analysis:**
   - Full IEEE 1584-2018 empirical incident energy equations ($E$ in $\text{cal/cm}^2$) and boundary calculations are explicitly excluded from v1.0 and flagged for future development, pending verified peer-reviewed industrial test cases.

5. **Frequency Support:**
   - Conductor AC reactance ($X_L$) and transformer impedances in the bundled database are normalized to $50\text{ Hz}$ distribution grids. For $60\text{ Hz}$ projects (e.g. North America, Saudi Arabia), frequency conversion scaling ($X_{60} = 1.2 \cdot X_{50}$) must be specified in the `ProjectDesignBasis`.

---

## 2. Ingestion & Data Layer Limitations

1. **Supported Ingestion Formats:**
   - Ingestion is currently optimized for structured CSV files (`sample_load_schedule.csv`, `sample_cable_schedule.csv`).
   - Complex multi-tab legacy Excel files (`.xlsx`) with merged cells, macros, or arbitrary visual formatting require pre-cleaning or export to standard CSV format prior to ingestion.

2. **Tenant Isolation:**
   - Multi-tenant data segregation is currently enforced at the logical schema level (`organisation_id`, `project_id`) within local JSON/memory data structures. Multi-tenant database partitioning (e.g. PostgreSQL Row Level Security) is not active in this offline release.

---

## 3. Deployment & Operational Limitations

1. **Deployment Architecture:**
   - Release `v1.0.0-rc1` is designed and packaged as a **local engineering application and visual test lab** (`demo_dashboard.html`).
   - It does not include an active web hosting container, public cloud backend, user authentication server, or billing gateway.
   - The live WordPress website at `proengineering.in` is strictly protected and remains unimpacted by this release.

2. **Statutory Engineering Responsibility:**
   - Outputs generated by this software provide deterministic mathematical verification and discrepancy flagging based strictly on user inputs.
   - Outputs do **NOT** constitute statutory certification, licensed Professional Engineer (PE) or Chartered Engineer (CEng) stamps, or official approval by standard-setting bodies (IEC, IEEE, BIS, NFPA, CEA).
   - The Engineer of Record remains solely responsible for plant engineering and safety compliance.
