## 1. Scope of Engagement

ProEngineering will perform an automated, deterministic design assurance review of Client's preliminary industrial radial distribution schedules using the **ProEngineering Integrated Power Design Assurance Engine**.

### Scope Inclusions (Up to 100 Radial Circuits):
1. **Design Current Verification:** Verification of continuous full-load current ($I_b$) against power factor and motor efficiency baselines.
2. **Voltage Drop Audit:** Calculation of operating temperature resistance ($R_{90}$), active/reactive line impedance, and running voltage drop ($\Delta V\%$) against Client's specified design basis.
3. **Transient Motor Inrush Dip Check:** Verification of motor starting bus voltage dip ($\le 10\%$ or $\le 15\%$) for motors $\ge 15\text{ kW}$.
4. **Protective Device Coordination Check:** Deterministic verification of $I_b \le I_n \le I_z'$ per IEC 60364-4-43 Clause 433.1.
5. **Adiabatic Short-Circuit Thermal Withstand:** Verification of conductor minimum cross-sectional area ($S_{min} = I_{sc}\sqrt{t}/k$) per IEC 60364-5-54 and IEC 60949.
6. **Cross-Document Discrepancy Matrix:** Detection of equipment tag orphans, rating mismatches, and conductor thermal overload risks.
