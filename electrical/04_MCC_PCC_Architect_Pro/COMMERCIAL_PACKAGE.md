## 2. Key Pain Points Solved

- **Eliminate Wrong Starter Selection:**
  Stop guessing between DOL, Star-Delta, Soft Starter, and VFD. The deterministic decision engine selects the optimal starting technology based on motor kW, process starting torque, and supply grid voltage dip constraints.
- **Physical Feasibility Out of the Box:**
  No more discovering at the factory floor that a $75\text{ kW}$ soft starter won't fit in a standard tier module. Modules are stacked into standardized vertical columns complying with IEC 61439-2 Form 3b/4b separation.
- **Complete Automation I/O Schedules Included:**
  Instant generation of all PLC/DCS control interface points (Run, Trip, Local/Remote, E-Stop, Current Transducers), saving electrical and automation teams dozens of coordination meetings.
