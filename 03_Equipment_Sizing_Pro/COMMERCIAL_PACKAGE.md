## 2. What Sets Equipment Sizing Pro Apart

- **Four-Point Cable Sizing Engine:**
  Most tools only check continuous ampacity. `Equipment Sizing Pro` rigorously tests continuous derated thermal current, steady-state voltage drop, motor starting transient dip, **and adiabatic short-circuit thermal withstand ($S_{min} = I_{sc}\sqrt{t}/k$)**. Never fail an inspector's fault-withstand audit again.
- **Commercial Standard Series Auto-Mapping:**
  Calculated sizes are automatically mapped to nearest standard commercial ratings (IEC 60076 for transformers, IEC 60947-2 for circuit breakers, and standard copper/aluminium mm² cross-sections).
- **Instant Procurement Datasheets:**
  Transform raw engineering requirements into formatted technical datasheets and comparison registers ready for RFQs and tender bid submissions.
