1. QUICKSTART INSTRUCTIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "launch_demo.bat" (or directly open "index.html" in Chrome, 
   Edge, Firefox, or Safari).
2. The dashboard will open immediately in your web browser.
3. No installation, software setup, Node.js, Python, or internet connection 
   is required. The suite runs 100% offline and in-memory.

2. DASHBOARD MODULES INCLUDED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tab 01: Feeder Voltage Drop & Inrush Transient Solver (IEC 60364-5-52)
- Tab 02: Power Transformer Sizing & Bolted Fault Capacity (IEC 60076)
- Tab 03: Adiabatic Short-Circuit Conductor Thermal Withstand (IEC 60364-5-54)
- Tab 04: Substation Grounding Tolerable Touch & Step Safety (IEEE Std 80 / IS 3043)
- Tab 05: Cross-Document Design Assurance & Discrepancy Matrix (Cross-Module Audit)

3. AUDIT REPORT DELIVERABLE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In Tab 05, click the "Open Watermarked Engineering Report (PDF/Print)" button 
to view or print the generated audit-ready deliverable:
- sample_verification_report.html
(Contains complete equations, input snapshots, and active findings register).

4. SAMPLE INDUSTRIAL DATASETS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two reference synthetic plant schedules are provided in this folder:
- sample_load_schedule.csv   (Plant equipment load schedule)
- sample_cable_schedule.csv  (Feeder cable & breaker protective schedule)
